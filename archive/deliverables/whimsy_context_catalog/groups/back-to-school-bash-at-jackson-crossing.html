Happening today at The Jackson Crossing Mall ! We're excited to welcome families to a calm, low-stimulation Back to school bash designed to make getting ready for the new school year a little easier and a lot more enjoyable. This event is perfect for families looking for a relaxed environment where children can: 🎒 Receive a FREE loaded backpack with a hot sandwich voucher (while supplies last) 🎟️ Receive Michigan Theatre Summer Kids Movie Series tickets 📸 Have their photos taken in a comfortable, low-pressure setting 🤩 Meet a fun Mascot and snap a selfie! Our goal is to create a welcoming, stress-free experience where families can prepare for the school year without the crowds and overwhelming atmosphere. We can't wait to see you at The Crossing, where community gathers!
