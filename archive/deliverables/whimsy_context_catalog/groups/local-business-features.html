Global Recognition Awards-Exceptional Achievement! "Kayli Pickett has been recognized with a 2026 Global Recognition Award for her sustained commitment to transforming The Jackson Crossing Mall into a genuine community anchor through exemplary leadership, measurable service outcomes, and creative media work that collectively reflect excellence in Customer Experience, Leadership, Service, and Artistic Accomplishment. Pickett serves as Media, Marketing, and Events Director at The Jackson Crossing Mall in Jackson County, where she has become a driving force behind the mall's remarkable recovery at a time when retail spaces across the United States continue to struggle with declining foot traffic and rising vacancies. Her work stands as a clear example of what focused, community-centered leadership can achieve when applied with consistency and purpose." Creativity is not a secondary function in Pickett's role. It is central to how she communicates the mall's identity and purpose. Through graphic design, photography, and targeted media campaigns, she documents and amplifies the stories of local businesses and nonprofit partnerships. This artistic dimension of her work does more than generate content; it shapes how the community perceives the mall and, by extension, how invested residents feel in its success. The visual and narrative outputs she produces serve a marketing function and a civic one, reinforcing the mall's standing as a place where people and organizations belong. "Alex Sterling, a spokesperson for Global Recognition Awards, acknowledged the significance of Pickett's recognition: "Kayli Pickett exemplifies the kind of leadership and service commitment that this award was designed to honor. Her ability to connect creative vision with genuine community impact, and to produce measurable results in a challenging industry environment, is precisely what separates exceptional contributors from the rest." Global Recognition Awards continues to identify individuals whose work raises the standard within their field, and Kayli Pickett's contributions to Jackson County's civic and commercial life make her a fitting recipient of this recognition. We are so proud to celebrate this amazing achievement and the impact Kay continues to make in our community! #theglobalrecognitionawards
